Built for SSD & NVMe Thermals

SB SSD TEMPS is a free, portable Windows application designed specifically for SSD and NVMe temperature monitoring. It was created through hands-on NVMe cooling testing and experimentation, with a focus on displaying useful drive thermal information without becoming another full SSD benchmarking suite.

SSD Temperature Monitoring Features

Multi-Drive Monitoring

Monitor connected SSD and NVMe drives simultaneously from a single interface.

Multiple Temperature Sensors

Display available Composite, Controller and NAND temperatures where those sensors are supported by the drive.

Thermal Thresholds

View firmware-reported warning, critical and thermal-management temperature information.

Thermal-Time History

Inspect available lifetime high-temperature information reported by the SSD or NVMe drive.

Live Drive Activity

Watch live read and write activity with tachometer-style displays and accurate numerical values.

Portable Windows Software

No traditional installation required. Download, run and monitor your storage temperatures.

More Than a Single SSD Temperature

An SSD can expose more thermal information than one simple temperature reading. Depending on the hardware, NVMe drives may report Composite, Controller and NAND temperatures along with firmware-defined thermal thresholds.

SB SSD TEMPS is focused on those details. The goal is to make drive thermal behavior easy to observe during normal use, heavy storage activity, cooling experiments and SSD evaluation.
